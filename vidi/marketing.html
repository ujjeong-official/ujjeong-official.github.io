Vidi

Your camera roll is full of videos you meant to sort out. Vidi makes that a few minutes of swiping instead of an afternoon.

✨ Features

  • One Video at a Time: Videos play full screen, one after another. Decide with a tap instead of guessing from thumbnails.
  • Keep or Trash: Every decision is one tap, and every decision can be undone.
  • It Remembers: Vidi never shows you the same clip twice. Clean up over several sittings and pick up exactly where you left off.
  • A Trash You Can Review: Nothing is deleted until you say so. Look through everything you marked, restore anything, then clear it all at once.
  • See What It Freed: Vidi shows you how much space your cleanup actually reclaimed.
  • Biggest First (Pro): The Photos app can't sort videos by file size. Vidi puts the space hogs at the front of the line.
  • Share Before You Let Go: Send a clip to a friend or save it elsewhere straight from the feed.

🔒 Your Videos Stay Yours

Vidi has no servers and no account. Your videos are never uploaded anywhere, and nothing you do in the app is tracked or analyzed. Everything happens on your iPhone.

💚 Free and Pro

  • Free: Review as many videos as you like, and delete up to 20 of them.
  • Vidi Pro: Unlimited deletions and biggest-first sorting, as a single one-time purchase. No subscription.

📱 Availability

Vidi is available for iPhone on the Apple App Store.
